## Target and proposed 40-minute flow

**Target:** I can combine geometric shapes and different lines to invent my own
creature. **Standard:** unverified; no code supplied.

| Minutes | Proposed action |
| --- | --- |
| 4 | Entry: revisit the existing sketch, identify shapes and consider a new arrangement. |
| 5 | Demonstration: compare two arrangements, then show straight, curved and zigzag lines. Teach the technique without requiring a copied creature. |
| 21 | Making: try two arrangements, choose a creature/pose/arrangement, and add varied lines. Use existing crayons only if stock and time permit. |
| 4 | Checkpoint: point to two line types and explain or demonstrate one arrangement choice. Use this evidence to choose the next small action. |
| 6 | Cleanup/re-entry: return crayons by container, save work, clear scraps and identify where to resume. |

**Total: 40 minutes.** A timer does not establish completion for every maker.
